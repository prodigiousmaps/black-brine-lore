# East Bank Guild Hall — Guild of Free Fishermen

## Overview
On Salt Crown’s polished docks, this hall handles permits, taxation, trade disputes, and public-facing guild business.

## Appearance
Whitewashed stone, oak beams, blue shutters, crossed-oars relief. Inside: lemon oil, mounted trophies, founder portraits, harbor views.

## Role in Society
Part labor office, part gentleman’s tavern, part shrine to honest work. Captains, foremen, and retired guildmasters meet merchants here.

## Features
- **Reception Hall:** Merchants and buyers meet guild reps.  
- **Guildmaster’s Office:** Harbor view; ship models and high ledgers.  
- **Council Room:** Negotiations and arbitration.  
- **Captain’s Lounge:** River rum and hot spiced eel broth.  
- **Market Quay:** Smoked/salted goods for wealthier clients.

## Notable Figures
- **Arven “Tidewise” Holt (Guildmaster):** Diplomatic, steady, quietly ruthless in negotiation.  
- **Sella Dorn (Clerk of Ledgers):** Meticulous half-elf; knows every debt.  
- **Olin Marr (Dock Steward):** Veteran; knows each hull by sound.

## Culture & Politics
Leans negotiation, regulation, and influence; sometimes clashes with Crosswater’s populist streak. Shared creed: “No net untended, no fisher left behind.”

## Rumors
- Arven plans to unify both halls under one charter.  
- Sella keeps a secret bribe ledger.  
- The Captain’s Lounge doubles as a private gambling den.  
- Late-night meetings linked to dockworker disappearances.
