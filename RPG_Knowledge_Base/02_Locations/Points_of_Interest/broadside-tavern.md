  The Broadside Tavern is more than a watering hole—it is a gaming hall, ale house, and steamy bath house all in one.
  Known for its multilevel drinking hall and nightly performances, it draws pirates, nobles, informants, and adventurers alike into its orbit.

geography: >
  Located in the northern district of Little Sedna, the Broadside is accessible by main street and dockside.
  Its ancient interior features a gaming floor lined with well-worn tables where citizens gather to play games of chance, strategy, and skill.
  A high mezzanine overlooks the gaming floor for those who prefer music and drinking.
