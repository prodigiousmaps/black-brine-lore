# West Bank Guild Hall — Guild of Free Fishermen

## Overview
The West Bank Guild Hall is the beating heart of the working fishers of Black Brine. It serves as the headquarters for the western half of the Guild of Free Fishermen, overseeing dock operations, catch negotiations, and community support for those who work the southern waters below the Red Chain Bridge.

## Appearance
A broad, timber-framed hall built directly onto the riverside pilings. The walls are draped in drying nets, flags of past fishing crews, and lanterns made from glass floats. Inside, the smell of ale and brine lingers under a ceiling blackened by years of smoke. Voices rise and fall in argument, laughter, and song.

## Role in Society
More than a workplace, this hall is a refuge and rally point. The guild functions like a mix between a labor union and a veterans’ hall—fishers gather to share profits, settle disputes, and raise tankards to the lost.  
When a member is injured, the guild helps their family; when someone dies at sea, the hall hosts the wake.

## Features
- **Guild Drinking Hall:** The social center, filled with long tables, ale kegs, and the sound of river shanties.  
- **Meeting Chamber:** Used for votes, contracts, and disciplinary hearings.  
- **Guildmaster’s Office:** Overlooks the docks and holds records of dues and disputes.  
- **Fish Market:** The street outside transforms daily into a crowded bazaar where citizens buy fresh catch directly from guild members.  
- **Working Docks:** Small boats unload their hauls here each morning before repairs and restocking.

## Notable Figures
- **Garrin “Grayhook” Darral (Guildmaster):** Grizzled veteran with an iron hook for a hand; respected for fairness and loyalty.  
- **Merra Tollin (Guild Clerk):** Sharp-tongued and efficient; tracks every coin and rumor with equal precision.

## Culture
The hall is loud, loyal, and united by the creed *“One river, one net.”* Fights break out as easily as songs, but grudges rarely last longer than the tide. The walls are lined with plaques honoring fishers lost to the deep.

## Adjacent Location — The Silver Minnow
Across the alley lies **The Silver Minnow**, a beloved riverside eatery known for its romantic booths and Helra Vinn’s famous brine-glazed trout.  
Fishermen spend their pay there, and travelers rent the three upstairs rooms—two small, one suite—for a view of the river lights.

## Rumors
- A sealed hatch beneath the hall leads to forgotten smuggler tunnels.  
- Merra Tollin keeps a secret ledger of illegal catches for hush money.  
- Garrin Grayhook once served the Council of Free Captains under another name.
