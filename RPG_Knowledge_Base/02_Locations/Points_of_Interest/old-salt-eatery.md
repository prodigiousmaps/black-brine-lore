# Old Salt Eatery

## Overview
A battered, tide-warped tavern perched above the Blackmire muck, the Old Salt Eatery is the district’s most reliable source of hot food, cheap grog, and whispered intelligence. It smells of burnt fish, cracked pepper, damp timber, and brine that never fully dries. The stew changes daily—sometimes subtly, sometimes dramatically—and the regulars insist that the flavor follows the mood of the tides.

## Appearance
Constructed from salvaged hull timber and old pier pilings, the building leans slightly toward the canal as if listening. Lanterns hang from tarred rope lines and sway in the fog. Inside, long communal tables dominate the main room. At the center stands the eel-tank bar—thick glass filled with restless blackmire eels that snap at passing fingers.

The floorboards are permanently salt-crusted and slope gently toward a drainage seam. During storms, the whole structure groans like a ship under strain. At high tide, water laps beneath the planks; at low tide, mud and forgotten debris are visible below.

## Role in Society
The Old Salt Eatery functions as unofficial neutral ground within Blackmire. No blades drawn. No overt spellcasting. Disputes step outside. Even rival captains respect the rule.

Beyond food and drink, the tavern serves as:
- A rumor exchange point.
- A quiet meeting place for envoys.
- A low-level information market brokered through implication rather than contracts.

Its continued neutrality helps stabilize Blackmire’s fragile balance of power.

## Features
- **Saltfather’s Stew:** A massive iron cauldron permanently fixed in the kitchen. Ingredients vary daily. The exact recipe is known only to the owner.
- **Eel Tank Bar:** Aggressive canal eels kept in a reinforced glass counter. Patrons tap the glass for luck before placing wagers.
- **Catch & Gamble:** A nightly betting game involving creatures trapped from the canal.
- **Curtained Booths:** Raised, partially enclosed seating used for private discussions.
- **Subfloor Hatch:** Bolted access to a partially flooded crawlspace. Few are permitted below.

## Notable Figures
- **Garrik “Salttooth” Vane (Owner):** One-eyed retired sea cook with a hook for a hand and filed teeth. Claims to have cooked for three pirate kings. Sharp memory, sharper instincts.
- **Krixie (Server):** Halfling with quick hands and perfect recall of faces, debts, and conversations.
- **Branwell (Regular):** Dockhand who is always present and always observing.
- **Elra “Soft-Voiced” (Singer):** Occasional performer whose sea laments quiet even the loudest tables.

## Rumors
- A regular customer was poisoned—deliberately or by accident.
- Vane keeps a treasure map etched onto a fishbone but refuses to sell it.
- The eels whisper at night when the canal falls silent.
- The stew contains a trace alchemical additive that enhances flavor—and perhaps more.
