# The Verdant Unmaking

## Overview
The Verdant Unmaking is a clandestine cult operating in the deep margins of Sedna, devoted to the belief that identity, morality, and order are false structures destined to rot away. Through repeated resurrection using a Far Realm organism known as the Returned Seed, adherents pursue progressive dissolution of the self, believing this decay brings them closer to a forgotten divine truth.

The cult claims to preserve suppressed doctrines of an erased god of rot and entropy, teaching that what survives repeated unmaking is closer to divinity than any ordered soul.

## Belief System
The cult teaches that:
- Structure is a lie imposed on a decaying cosmos
- Identity is a temporary arrangement of matter and memory
- Repeated resurrection strips away false form
- Chaos without restraint is the purest state of being

Advanced stages of degradation are revered as spiritual ascent. Violence, instability, and loss of self are celebrated as signs of sacred progress rather than failure.

## The Forgotten God
The Verdant Unmaking traces its doctrine to the annihilated faith of Moander, an erased god of rot, uncontrolled growth, and entropy. The cult does not claim Moander yet lives as a god, but that his truth persists wherever decay continues without purpose.

Moander is not prayed to for guidance. He is invoked as inevitability.

## The Returned Seed
The cult uses a non-sentient Far Realm organism capable of reconstructing dead hosts. Each resurrection rebuilds the body imperfectly, pouring more chaotic influence into the host and accelerating mental, emotional, and physical degradation.

The Seed is revered as an instrument, not a deity.

## The Spiral of Putrefaction
Cult doctrine recognizes six resurrection thresholds:
1. **The Seeded** – Recently Returned, largely intact
2. **The Softened** – Emotional instability, early memory erosion
3. **The Sloughing** – Identity collapse, volatility, aberrant capability
4. **The Verdant Feral** – Violent, ritual-responsive only
5. **The Blooming Horror** – Aberrant physiology, near-divine veneration
6. **The Compost** – Total loss of self; regarded as apotheosis

Higher thresholds are treated with greater reverence and protection.

## Practices and Rites
- Seeds are accepted voluntarily and kept until sleep
- Death is never induced; resurrection must be earned through fate
- Advanced Returned are never commanded, only released
- Execution of a Returned is forbidden, regardless of danger

Ferals are kept hidden and unleashed only against enemies of the cult.

## Organization
Leadership is composed primarily of mid-stage adherents who retain enough coherence to plan and conceal operations. Those who advance too far are removed from leadership roles and elevated to symbolic or ritual status.

## Role in Sedna
Publicly unknown, the Verdant Unmaking operates beneath forgotten shrines, drowned ruins, and sealed vaults. Rumors of feral attackers, impossible survivals, and cult-linked disappearances circulate among dockworkers, clergy, and magistrates, though proof remains elusive.

## Notable Figures
- **The Verdant Speaker:** A Stage-3 adherent whose fragmented proclamations form the cult’s living scripture.
- **The Keepers of Bloom:** Handlers responsible for containing and venerating advanced-stage Returned.

## Rumors
- Some cult members seek death repeatedly in rapid succession to hasten ascent.
- A Stage-6 Compost is said to have rooted itself beneath Sedna, warping the stone.
- Clergy investigating the cult often vanish before formal accusations are made.
