# Ships
- 3 cursed vessels, including their flagship: The Miasma
- Ghost-haunted, disease-ridden, creaking things covered in barnacles and rot.

# Crew
- Warped, mutated, and terrifying sailors.
- Some undead. Others worse.
- Known to chant in strange tongues and perform rites at sea.
- Deserters occasionally wash ashore blind or raving.

# Style
- Terror tactics, eldritch magic, and unpredictable cruelty
- Uses psychological warfare, ritualized fear, and contaminating plagues
- Captures ships not for loot—but for experiments or rituals

# Reputation
- The only fleet feared more dead than alive
- Those captured are rarely seen again—or return… wrong
- Rumors say they make “salt-walkers”—souls bound to their ship hulls

# Specialties
- Necrotic rituals, disease spreading, ghost binding
- Leaving cursed wrecks as messages to rivals
- Often seen where the fog is thickest

# Notoriety
- Accused of sinking dozens of merchant and pirate ships alike
- Even Rosy gives them a wide berth when their ships pull into port
- A standing superstition: “If the eye sees you, count your days.”

# Customs
- Ritual Branding: New crewmates are branded with a blackened eye tattoo somewhere on their body.
- Dead Tithes: Every ship in the fleet keeps a sealed coffin onboard. Its contents are unknown—and never opened.
- No Lanterns: They sail with no lights. Only eerie phosphorescent paint guides their hands.
- Shrouded Flag: They hoist black sails when they come to port. Locals stay inside.
