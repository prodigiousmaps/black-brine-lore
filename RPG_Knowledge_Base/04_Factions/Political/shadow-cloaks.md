# Shadow Cloak Agents

## Overview
Highly trained operatives handling investigations, intelligence gathering, and sensitive enforcement actions.

## Appearance
Form-fitting black leather armor, masked from the nose down, minimal markings, practical and intimidating.

## Role in Society
They investigate crimes, uncover conspiracies, and interrogate suspects. When they arrive, a situation is no longer routine.

## Features
- Covert surveillance networks
- Advanced interrogation methods
- Authority to override local enforcement

## Notable Figures
