# Tide Wardens

## Overview
A dispersed order tasked with confronting supernatural threats and maintaining ancient balances beyond the city.

## Appearance
Weathered leathers, oilskin cloaks, bone and stone ward charms, gear marked by salt and time.

## Role in Society
They operate outside the city, dealing with corruption, relics, and disturbances tied to deeper forces. Their authority supersedes local law when invoked.

## Features
- Autonomous territorial wardens
- Knowledge of ancient rites and wards
- Rapid, decisive intervention methods

## Notable Figures
- **Sable Marr (Tide Warden):** Oversees the Central Valley of East Sedna.

## Rumors
- They do not arrest—only resolve.
- They answer to older laws than the city itself.
