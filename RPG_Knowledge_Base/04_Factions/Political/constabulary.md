# Black Brine Constabulary

## Overview
The visible arm of law in Black Brine, tasked with maintaining order in the streets, docks, and public spaces.

## Appearance
Dark blue coats, salt-stained leather armor, tricorn hats, pistols and short swords standard issue.

## Role in Society
They enforce day-to-day law, protect commerce, and respond to disturbances. They defer complex investigations to the House of Questions.

## Features
- District-based command structure
- Strong dockside presence
- Reliance on numbers and coordinated response

## Notable Figures
- **High Constable:** Oversees city-wide operations.

## Rumors
- Some units are quietly on merchant payrolls.
- Certain districts enforce “selective law.”
