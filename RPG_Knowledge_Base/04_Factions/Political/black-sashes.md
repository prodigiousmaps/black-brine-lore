# The Black Sashes

## Overview
The Black Sashes are a decentralized intelligence and subversive network operating across Black Brine. They do not present themselves as a formal organization, instead existing as a series of loosely connected cells bound by shared philosophy and coded communication.

Their purpose is not conquest, but disruption—undermining systems that concentrate power while redistributing influence to those who operate outside the control of fleets, merchants, and centralized authority.

## Structure
The Black Sashes operate through independent cells with no publicly recognized leadership.

- Cells function autonomously.
- Members rarely know operatives outside their immediate circle.
- Communication relies on coded signals, rumor chains, and symbolic markers.
- Leadership, if it exists, is deliberately obscured.

This structure allows the organization to persist despite investigation or suppression.

## Ideology
The Black Sashes are guided by three core principles:

- **Knowledge is Power:** Information is the most valuable currency in Black Brine.
- **Silence is a Weapon:** Influence is best applied without recognition.
- **Control Must Be Broken:** Systems that consolidate power must be weakened or reshaped.

They are not anarchists, but they resist rigid hierarchies and exploitative control.

## Methods
The Black Sashes operate indirectly, favoring influence over force.

- Manipulating rumor and public perception
- Disrupting trade routes and scrip flow
- Supporting labor coordination and strikes
- Embedding operatives within guilds, markets, and institutions
- Using coded graffiti and symbolic identifiers

Their actions often appear coincidental unless viewed as part of a larger pattern.

## Role in Society
To the general population, the Black Sashes are rumor—an explanation for events that feel too coordinated to be chance.

Among laborers and dockworkers, they are seen as a quiet ally.

Among merchants and officials, they are viewed as a destabilizing threat.

Their presence creates a constant undercurrent of uncertainty within the city’s power structure.

## Notable Figures
- **Weighmaster Revine:** A covert operative embedded within labor and supply networks, known for subtle manipulation of economic outcomes.

## Rumors
- The Black Sashes maintain a hidden ledger of every major debt in the city.
- Some claim they can extinguish entire trade routes without ever appearing.
- A sash left at a doorway is said to mark a target for disruption—or protection.
