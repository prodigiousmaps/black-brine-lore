# Ships
- Numerous warships, sloops, and converted galleons
- All painted in deep violet and gold, trimmed with seaweed braiding and bone charms
- Flagships rumored to be blessed by Sedna herself—with hulls resistant to fire and salt

# Crew
- Devoted to Sedna, indoctrinated from youth or converted through blood oaths
- Mix of pirates, fanatics, and reformed heretics
- Known for singing eerie sea-hymns during battle

# Style
- Orthodox, brutal, and divine
- Often use Sedna-blessed weapons, religious fervor, and overwhelming force
- Never retreats. Never parley. Only surrender through sacrifice.

# Reputation
- Unchallenged rulers of Black Brine’s waters
- Rosy’s word is law—even rival captains hold their tongue
- Known for dragging captured crews before the altar in City Hull for ritual judgment

# Specialties
- Holy warfare, island defense, Sednaite enforcement
- Enslaving or converting enemy crews
- Escorting Black Brine’s most sacred or dangerous cargoes
- Unmatched expertise with blackpowder cannon and musket 

# Customs & Culture
- The Salt Baptism: All new sailors must undergo a ritual drowning and revival—"to die to the self, and live for Sedna."
- Tide Blades: Each crew member carries a short curved dagger carved from reefbone, sacred to Sedna.
- Prayer Before Fire: Cannons are fired only after invocation—“Her waves break first”
- Offerings at Sea: Every full moon, each ship sends an offering to Sedna overboard—coins, blood, teeth, secrets.
