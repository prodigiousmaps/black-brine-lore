# Black Brine Trade Authority

## Overview
The Black Brine Trade Authority (TA) is a quasi-governmental institution that
controls currency, regulates key resources, and licenses exchange within Black
Brine and its surrounding territories. Publicly framed as a neutral stabilizing
force, it functions in practice as the primary mechanism by which trade is
converted into political power.

## Mandate
- Issue and maintain confidence in **Trade Authority scrip**.
- License and operate **Exchange Houses** for coin-to-scrip conversion.
- Regulate strategic resources critical to city stability.
- Maintain gold and material reserves for mainland settlement.
- Coordinate investigations into counterfeiting and market manipulation.

## Scrip
**Physical Form**
- Copper discs, approximately quarter-sized.
- Embossed with the Trade Authority emblem.
- Punched with a small hole so scrip can be strung or tied to goods.

**Economic Function**
- Scrip’s value exceeds its raw materials due to controlled issuance.
- Required for many legal transactions within Black Brine.
- Used to regulate wages, prices, and access to commerce.

## Exchange System
**Exchange Houses**
Official facilities throughout the city that convert mainland coinage into
Trade Authority scrip, serving as the primary gateway into the local economy.

**Paper Notes**
High-value paper instruments issued through lending houses to facilitate
large transactions without the physical movement of coin.

## Gold Policy
**Incentives**
- Preferential acceptance of scrip over gold in daily commerce.

**The Great Exchange Day**
An annual amnesty holiday during which citizens may exchange gold for scrip
without fees, encouraging the clearing of private gold reserves.

**Gold Reserves**
- Settlements with mainland trade partners.
- Strategic leverage during shortages, crises, or supply shocks.

## Regulated Resources
**Mistglass**
- Easy to mine, difficult to smelt mineral.
- Critical ingredient in scrip production.
- Heavily regulated to protect currency integrity.

**Deepmoss Resin**
- Volatile biological resin with industrial and alchemical uses.
- Refinement produces toxic byproducts requiring controlled disposal.

## Enforcement
Enforcement activities are conducted in cooperation with the
**House of Questions**, focusing on:
- Counterfeiting operations.
- Market manipulation and diversion.
- Ledger audits and merchant compliance.

## Structure
**Chancellery of Scrip**
- Scrip issuance and reserve policy.
- Audits and anti-counterfeit coordination.

**Exchange House Directorate**
- Licensing and oversight of Exchange Houses.
- Teller discipline and emergency transaction freezes.

**Weights and Measures Office**
- Verification standards.
- Merchant compliance enforcement.

**Resource Compliance Bureau**
- Mistglass regulation.
- Deepmoss Resin oversight.
- Hazardous byproduct enforcement.

**Dock and Cargo Assessment Desk**
- Manifest intake and valuation.
- Contraband screening coordination.

## Role in Society
**Citizens**
The Trade Authority provides wage stability and food security while quietly
enforcing dependence on scrip.

**Merchants**
Acts as both gatekeeper and arbiter of legitimate value.

**Dissidents**
Viewed as the vulnerable center of power—if money fails, authority follows.

## Notable Figures
- **Chancellor of Scrip:** Senior official overseeing currency policy and
  anti-counterfeiting enforcement; works closely with the House of Questions,
  creating political tension due to inquisitorial ties to the fleets.
- **Exchange House High Teller:** Regional manager responsible for transaction
  oversight and freezes.
- **Resource Compliance Warden:** Authority over inspections, seizures, and
  environmental enforcement.

## Rumors
- Trade Authority ledgers predict market shocks before they occur.
- Some Exchange Houses maintain secret secondary books for favored clients.
- A reserve shortfall is being concealed to prevent panic.
