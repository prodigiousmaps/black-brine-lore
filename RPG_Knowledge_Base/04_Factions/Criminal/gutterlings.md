# Overview
The Gutterlings are a street gang of orphaned and runaway children who survive through pickpocketing, distraction tricks, and swarming tactics. Though often underestimated by adults, they are feared by rival gangs for their loyalty and ability to overwhelm targets in groups.

# History
Formed by children left behind by the great fleets, the Gutterlings carved out a niche as the eyes and ears of the city’s alleys. Their informal leadership comes from an older sibling figure who "aged out" but never left the crew.

# Structure & Culture
- Signals: whistles, chalk markings, hand gestures
- Weapons: slingshots, stolen knives, improvised tools
- Code: “No Gutterling left behind”
- Superstition: If they spit in your drink, you’re marked — expect to be stripped bare within the week.

# Allies & Rivals
- Allies: sometimes courted by rival gangs as spies, but their loyalty is inward.
- Rivals: rival gangs who underestimate them often pay dearly.

# Adventure Hooks
- The PCs are swarmed and pickpocketed — but one Gutterling slips them a secret note.
- A kidnapped Gutterling begs the party to rescue them before the gang retaliates.
