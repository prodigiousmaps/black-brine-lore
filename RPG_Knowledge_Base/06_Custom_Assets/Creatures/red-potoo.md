# Description
Strange entities shaped like potoo birds, formed from blood-red mist with glowing white eyes. They perch silently on rooftops, never making a sound, never interacting, and vanish the instant they are noticed.

# Superstitions
- Gamblers: Sight before play means certain loss.
- Lovers: Portent of betrayal within the week.
- Sailors: Omens of storms, curses of the Hollow Goddess.
- Children: “Ghost scouts” of Sedna, searching for liars.

# Theories
- Astral projections of a forgotten cult.
- Wards left from the goddess war.
- Mass hallucinations triggered by ancient magic.

# Adventure Hooks
- PCs tasked to catch one — but what happens if they succeed?
- A noble pays for a rooftop watch when Red Potoos appear above their manor.
