sections:
  - id: 1
    name: "Gear Modifications for Maximum Stealth"
    description: >
      Techniques for reducing visibility, sound, and reflection through strategic alterations
      to clothing, armor, and weapons.
    categories:
      - title: "Clothing & Armor Adjustments"
        items:
          - "Muted colors and camouflage using jungle moss, crushed berries, and charcoal."
          - "Silencing metal gear: avoid chainmail or plate; wrap all metal parts in cloth or soft leather."
          - "Padded footwear (moss or soft leather) to dampen footsteps."
          - "Secure loose items—wrap rations and supplies in waxed cloth; use gourd containers instead of metal flasks."
          - "Dull reflective surfaces with ash or charcoal; coat blades in blackened oil."
          - "Favor bows, daggers, or bone knives over firearms or heavy melee weapons."
  
  - id: 2
    name: "Scent Masking & Suppression"
    description: >
      Predators smell before they see; masking scent is essential for survival in the deep jungle.
    categories:
      - title: "Jungle Scent Techniques"
        items:
          - "Use crushed herbs, moss, or oils to cover scent; dust with damp earth or charcoal."
          - "Avoid perfume, strong soap, and smoke; extinguish fires carefully to prevent soot smell."
          - "Extreme measure: smear local animal blood to confuse predators and trackers."

  - id: 3
    name: "Movement & Travel Precautions"
    description: >
      Stealth in motion—how to travel unseen and unheard through dangerous terrain.
    categories:
      - title: "Tactical Movement Adjustments"
        items:
          - "Move at half speed to gain advantage on Stealth checks."
          - "Avoid dry leaves and twigs; step on moss or solid stone."
          - "Use hand signals and pre-arranged gestures for communication."
          - "Stay close to cover and shadows when advancing."
          - "Plan movement during gusts or rainfall to mask sound."

  - id: 4
    name: "Campsite Stealth & Fire Discipline"
    description: >
      Proper rest practices for stealth missions; minimizing exposure while encamped.
    categories:
      - title: "Safe Camping Procedures"
        items:
          - "No open flames; use buried embers or hot stones for heat."
          - "Choose hidden campsites within dense vegetation or between fallen logs."
          - "Scatter food scraps and bury waste far from camp."
          - "Use false camps and decoy fires to mislead pursuers."

  - id: 5
    name: "Weapon & Combat Readiness for Silent Kills"
    description: >
      Combat should be fast, quiet, and decisive—noise is death.
    categories:
      - title: "Silent Kill Tactics"
        items:
          - "Always strike from stealth to gain surprise."
          - "Neutralize loud threats or casters first."
          - "Use daggers, bows, and garrotes instead of firearms when possible."
          - "Firearms: shoot from cover, then immediately relocate."
          - "Employ hit-and-run tactics—strike, vanish, reappear elsewhere."
          - "Use thrown objects or mimic jungle sounds to distract enemies."

  - id: 6
    name: "Psychological Warfare Against the Jungle’s Hunters"
    description: >
      The jungle whispers; its predators test resolve. Survival demands mental endurance.
    categories:
      - title: "Mental Fortitude & Countermeasures"
        items:
          - "Never respond to the whispering fog or disembodied voices."
          - "Carry fire but conceal it—predators fear light, yet fire attracts others."
          - "Mark paths subtly using natural markers, not carvings or chalk symbols."
          - "Do not stray from the group; those who walk alone in the western jungle do not return."

  - id: 7
    name: "Final Checklist Before Entering the Jungle"
    checklist:
      - "Armor and gear wrapped in cloth for silence."
      - "Clothing camouflaged with mud and plant dyes."
      - "Weapons and tools secured against clinking."
      - "Scent masked with herbs, charcoal, and blood."
      - "Hand signals established for communication."
      - "Fire preparation without visible trace."
      - "Supplies packed tightly to prevent rattling."
      - "Final warning: The jungle watches. Do not leave tracks."
