# Raif Lockwood (“Dead Weight”)

## Overview
Raif Lockwood is a discharged rifle officer who survives by being useful without being visible. He works security on ships and overland caravans, fixes firearms for locals, and avoids faction entanglements. Veterans recognize him immediately. Most others underestimate him.

## Background
Born to minor gentry and pressed into service early, Raif served with the **9th March Rifles**, known as the *Marching Dead*. He specialized in rear-guard actions and holding ground under pressure. His habit of waiting until action truly mattered earned him the nickname “Dead Weight.” Peace ended his usefulness, and he drifted into contract work before settling in Black Brine.

## Appearance
Tall, broad, and deliberate. Wears a patched land-service greatcoat and a reshaped tricorne to clear his horns. Carries a long musket with bayonet and what appears to be an unusually long knife. Looks prepared rather than threatening.

## Armament & Skills
- Gunderson musket (“Late Issue”), flintlock pistol, bayonet
- Gunsmithing (smith’s tools reflavored)
- Strong, athletic, perceptive, survival-focused
- Uses thaumaturgy only in mundane, practical ways
- Fire resistance; restrained infernal magic

## Current Life
Raif rents a room on **Fog House Row** in Blackmire. He drinks at **Tipsy Fens**, reads pulp adventure serials at the Black Brine Library, and contributes to the neighborhood by fixing guns, cutting wood, or lending muscle when asked. Harbor masters and guild members know how to find him.

## Reputation
- **Locals:** Quiet, reliable, pays his way  
- **Sailors:** Dead weight—until he isn’t  
- **Veterans:** A nod, and no questions  

## Motivations
- Protection of people and places under his watch
- Useful obscurity over recognition
- Honest tools and fair exchange

## Rumors

- “That knife isn’t a knife.”
- “If he’s still smoking, you’re not in real trouble yet.”
