# Description
A cheerful eccentric who offers free carriage rides to anyone willing to listen to his relentless sponsor’s sales pitch. Garrick rotates sponsors weekly, ranging from fishmongers to crime bosses, often contradicting himself mid-ride.

# Personality
- Enthusiastic, loud, never malicious.
- Loves gossip and rumor-spreading.
- Treats his passengers like “customers,” even when they groan.

# Hooks
- His sponsor’s products sometimes carry secret codes or smuggled goods.
- Rivals sabotage Garrick’s cart, dragging the PCs into the fallout.
- He can unwittingly drop critical plot hooks in his rambling pitches.
