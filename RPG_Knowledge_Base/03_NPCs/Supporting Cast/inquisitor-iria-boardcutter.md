# Inquisitor Boardcutter

## Overview
Heartless and unpredictable, tortures with her blades, gets answers with blunt threats, and solves crime like it's war. Keeps a sack of dried ears as “reminders.” Smells like salt and blood. Everyone avoids her laugh.
