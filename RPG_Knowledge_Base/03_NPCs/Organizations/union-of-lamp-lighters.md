# Overview
A secretive guild responsible for lighting and maintaining the city’s whale-oil lamps. They patrol the streets at night, carrying keys and oil, with whistles that serve as coded signals. Their reach gives them unmatched knowledge of the city after dark.

# Culture
- Lamp patterns rumored to carry coded messages.
- Whistle-calls mark warnings and shifts.
- They claim neutrality but can cripple a district by leaving lamps unlit.

# Superstitions
- An unlit crossroads lamp means someone important will die that week.
- Locals leave offerings at lamp posts for safe passage.

# Adventure Hooks
- PCs hired to guard lamp lighters threatened by gangs.
- PCs notice lamps forming deliberate patterns across the city.
- A murdered lamp lighter leaves behind an encoded route map.
