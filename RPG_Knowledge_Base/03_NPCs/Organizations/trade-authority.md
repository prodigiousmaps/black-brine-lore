# Trade Authority of Black Brine  
## Structure, Power, and Function in Sedna’s Island Economy

---

## Overview

The Trade Authority (TA) is the economic heart and regulatory backbone of Black Brine.  
It oversees all commerce, currency, tariffs, licensing, and import/export activity on the island.  
In a city driven by piracy, unpredictable magic, and aggressively competitive markets, the Trade Authority stands as the closest thing to a stabilizing government force.

Their influence touches every citizen—dockhands, artisans, merchants, tavern owners, smugglers, and guilds alike.

---

## Core Mandates

The Trade Authority maintains **five central responsibilities**.

---

## 1. Regulate Currency (Scrip System)

The TA mints and manages **scrip**, the island’s primary currency. They monitor:

- alloy purity and minting standards  
- anti-counterfeiting measures  
- market circulation rates  
- Exchange House operations  
- annual gold conversion events (Great Exchange Day)  

Because the TA controls scrip, it effectively controls the economic lifeblood of the city.

---

## 2. Administer Tariffs and Duties

All goods entering or leaving Black Brine—legal or otherwise—pass through TA assessment. This includes:

- cargo manifests  
- trade vessel registrations  
- harbor docking fees  
- merchant licensing  
- import/export taxes  
- confiscation or fines for discrepancies  

For many, TA tariffs are the single most predictable cost of doing business.

---

## 3. Monitor Economic Crimes

The TA works closely with the **House of Questions**, especially the **Ledger Spire** cell, to investigate:

- counterfeit scrip  
- fraudulent manifests  
- smuggling networks  
- illegal minting tools  
- price-fixing and market manipulation  
- black-market commodities such as Deepmoss Resin  

Where the TA identifies an anomaly, the House of Questions provides forceful enforcement.

---

## 4. Maintain Reserve Wealth

Physical gold is tightly restricted on Sedna. Citizens and businesses must convert most gold into scrip except where needed for:

- foreign trade  
- merchant reserve requirements  
- contracted mainland shipments  

Collected gold is stored in TA vaults and allocated for:

- scrip stability  
- economic emergencies  
- minting tools  
- truth-weight testing stones  
- high-level financial ritual work  

The **Great Exchange Day** sweeps excess gold from private hands and reasserts TA dominance.

---

## 5. Maintain Predictable Market Function

While the island thrives on chaos, markets cannot. The TA:

- monitors price fluctuations  
- tracks labor movement  
- regulates Mistglass, Deepmoss Resin, and Brineheart Root industries  
- sets commodity standards  
- prevents economic collapse from pirate-wealth surges  

This supports a measure of stability for guilds, artisans, and working families.

---

## Why the Trade Authority Matters in Daily Life

For the average citizen, the TA is both **protector** and **oppressor**.

They provide:
- secure, standard currency  
- price consistency  
- commercial fairness (in theory)  
- exchange services for mainland coin  
- merchant stability  

They demand:
- compliance  
- licensing fees  
- adherence to scrip-only systems  
- transparency in trade  
- acceptance of audits  

Failure to cooperate brings fines—or intervention from the House of Questions.
