# Black Brine — Key Exports

## Overview
Black Brine exports emphasize durability, scarcity control, and function under extreme conditions. Raw abundance is secondary to processed reliability.

## Appearance
Export goods are typically sealed, stamped, and standardized—barrels, crates, and resin-marked containers bearing Trade Authority insignia.

## Role in Society
Exports are the city’s primary leverage. Control of export licensing determines power, influence, and access to foreign markets.

## Features
- **Brineheart Products:** Whole hearts, pressed rations, and concentrates.
- **Refined Resin:** Fuels, sealants, binding agents, and alchemical bases.
- **Mist Glass:** Rare, mist-formed glass used in optics and ritual apparatus.
- **Preserved Goods:** Salted fish, meats, and brined vegetation.
- **Ship Supplies:** Rope, barrels, pitch, and treated materials.
- **Salvage Goods:** Reclaimed metals, tools, and refitted equipment.
- **Logistical Services:** Charts, manifests, and risk brokerage.

## Notable Figures
- **Scrip Policy Official (Trade Authority):** Oversees valuation pressures that indirectly shape export flow and pricing.

## Rumors
- Certain resin grades are quietly restricted due to volatile or arcane properties.
- Mist glass shipments occasionally vanish without record.
- Brineheart concentrates may have undocumented effects when consumed long-term.
