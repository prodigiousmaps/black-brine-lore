# Scrip in Black Brine  
## Currency, Control, and Daily Life on Sedna

---

## What Is Scrip?

Scrip is the official currency of Black Brine—small, copper-based discs embossed with the Trade Authority seal and drilled with a hole so they can be strung together.

Each piece is:

- standardized  
- difficult to forge  
- easy to transport  
- widely recognized across Sedna  

Though it contains very little intrinsic value, scrip is the backbone of the island’s economy.

---

## Why Scrip Exists

Black Brine sits at the crossroads of piracy, privateering, and mercantile trade. Ships arrive carrying:

- gold  
- foreign coin  
- stolen treasure  
- unstable inflows of wealth  

If gold circulated freely, the local economy would collapse each time a pirate unloaded a fortune.

Scrip stabilizes the island by:

- replacing gold for most domestic transactions  
- allowing the Trade Authority to regulate flow and value  
- preventing sudden wealth spikes from destabilizing markets  
- making currency traceable and accountable  

---

## How Scrip Is Backed

Scrip has little intrinsic metal value. It is backed by:

- the Trade Authority’s enforcement capabilities  
- taxation and tariff revenue  
- control of major exports such as Mistglass and Deepmoss Resin  
- strict anti-counterfeiting measures  
- the island’s dependence on a standardized currency  

Scrip retains value because the Trade Authority compels the economy to use it.

---

## How Scrip Moves Through Society

### Everyday Money
Scrip is used for:

- food  
- taverns  
- rent  
- tools  
- ship repairs  
- artisans’ work  

Almost all small-scale commerce relies entirely on scrip.

### Taxation & Tariffs
The TA requires scrip payment for:

- docking fees  
- licenses  
- import/export taxes  
- fines  
- merchant audits  

This keeps the Authority firmly in control of economic function.

### Guild & Labor Payments
Guilds, crews, dockworkers, and artisans are paid primarily in scrip, reinforcing a cycle of dependence on TA-regulated currency.

### Underground Uses
Two major factions manipulate scrip:

- **Black Sashes** — counterfeit scrip to sabotage markets.  
- **Butchers** — lend scrip at predatory rates to control neighborhoods.  

Scrip is more than money—it is leverage.

---

## Gold vs. Scrip in Daily Life

### Gold Is Restricted
Citizens may possess small amounts of gold, but they cannot use it openly in most domestic transactions.

Using gold instead of scrip:

- incurs heavy fees  
- flags you for audit  
- invites scrutiny from the House of Questions  

### Exchange Houses
Foreign gold must be exchanged for scrip at intentionally unfavorable rates.

### Merchant Gold Reserves
Only merchants, ship captains, and trade guilds may hold substantial gold reserves for foreign trade. All such reserves are documented by the TA.

---

## The Great Exchange Day

Once per year, the Trade Authority offers a **fee-free gold-to-scrip conversion day** to:

- encourage citizens to surrender gold  
- purge the economy of foreign currency  
- replenish TA reserves  
- identify individuals with suspicious wealth  

Collected gold is stored in TA vaults, used for economic stability, minting tools, and truth weights.

---

## Anti-Counterfeiting Measures

Scrip pieces contain:

- Mistglass alloy traces  
- unique tarnish density profiles  
- precise hole beveling  
- batch-number variations visible only to TA inspectors  
- slow-tarnish oil that ages predictably  

Merchants and TA officers use verification methods ranging from visual checks to metallurgical tests.

Counterfeiting scrip is a **capital offense**, jointly investigated by the TA and the House of Questions.

---

## Social Impact of Scrip

### 1. Economic Stability
Scrip prevents runaway inflation caused by unpredictable pirate wealth.

### 2. Social Control
Because the TA controls currency, they control:

- business formation  
- ship operation  
- economic mobility  

Currency doubles as governance.

### 3. Class Striations
The wealthy store gold in TA vaults.  
The poor handle only scrip.  
Dependence on standardized currency deepens class divides.

### 4. Political Tension
The Black Sashes describe scrip as:

“**A chain disguised as coin.**”

Their efforts undermine its legitimacy; the TA defends it with ferocity.
