# Directive 11.4.2  
## Rights & Procedures Regarding Ownership of a Discovered Vessel  
Issued by the Harbor Inquisitoriate under mandate of the Trade Authority of Black Brine.

---

## I. Rights of Persons Who Bring a Vessel into Harbor

Any group or individual who delivers a vessel to Black Brine under unusual, hazardous, or unexplained circumstances is granted the following rights:

### 1. Right to Testify
All persons may present:
- their account of events  
- injuries sustained  
- reasons for assuming control of the vessel  

Testimony must be delivered truthfully under penalty of harbor law.

### 2. Right to Protection
While under investigation:
- individuals are entitled to safety from retaliation  
- Quiet Cloaks may be assigned to prevent tampering or coercion  

### 3. Right to Provisional Movement
Individuals may:
- remain aboard  
- be held at dock  
- be escorted into the city  

Movement is permitted only after an initial determination by the presiding Inquisitor.

### 4. Right to Salvage Claim
If:
- the vessel is ownerless, **or**  
- the rightful owner is confirmed dead without heirs,  

…the finders may lodge a **Formal Salvage Petition** with the Trade Authority.

---

## II. Determination of Ownership

Ownership of a maritime vessel is adjudicated under the following principles:

### 1. Priority of Living Owner
If any living person is recognized as:
- the legal captain of record, or  
- the registered owner of the vessel,  

…the ship must be returned to them regardless of circumstance.

### 2. Claims Must Be Proven
Any claimant must produce:
- registry papers  
- purchase ledgers  
- prior manifests  
- shipyard certificates  
- corroborating witness testimony  

Forgery carries severe penalties.

### 3. Death of Owner
If the captain or owner is deceased, the House of Questions must:
- verify cause of death  
- determine presence of heirs  
- evaluate debts or criminal liens  
- confirm no competing claims exist  

If no heir exists, the vessel becomes property of the Trade Authority.

### 4. Investigative Hold
No party may assume ownership until:
- all investigations conclude  
- the vessel is fully inspected  
- magical contamination is ruled out  
- cargo is accounted for  
- crew deaths are adjudicated  

Rusk’s false claim will trigger a mandatory, extended investigation.

---

## III. Rights to Salvage Reward

If the vessel is returned to a rightful owner, the rescuing group may receive:

### 1. Finder’s Fee
Typically:
- 5–15% of ship value  
- determined by risk, damage, and honesty of testimony  

### 2. Reward for Recovery of Cargo
If cargo is intact:
- additional scrip bounty  
- proportional to value and type  

Illegal cargo disqualifies this reward unless disclosed voluntarily.

### 3. Recommendation for Harbor Trust Status
Parties who demonstrate usefulness, truthfulness, and discipline may be approved for:
- reduced docking fees  
- permission to lease mooring space  
- future salvage contracts  

---

## IV. Temporary Custodial Stewardship

If the Trade Authority finds the rescuing party trustworthy but ownership cannot be confirmed immediately, the vessel may be assigned to them under **Provisional Stewardship**, meaning:

- the party may operate the vessel  
- but may not sell, alter, rename, or export it  
- and must present the vessel when summoned  
- and must submit monthly logs to the Harbor Office  
- and must surrender the vessel if rightful ownership is later proven  

Violation of custodial terms is considered **theft of Trade Authority property**.

---

## V. Inadmissible Claim Conditions

A group may **not** claim a vessel if:

- they murdered the rightful crew  
- they seized the ship unlawfully  
- the ship carries forbidden cargo  
- the ship is magically contaminated  
- harbor law violations occurred during approach  
- evidence contradicts their testimony  

Arbitration of these conditions falls to **Zunizar** and **Plagueglass**.
