overview: |
  A popular street and tavern game, exclusive to Black Brine. Played casually with chalk circles and seashells,
  or with carved bone tokens and fine boards in gambling dens. The game is simple to learn, fast to play,
  and steeped in superstition—locals claim that winning three rounds in a row earns the “crow’s blessing,”
  though what that means depends on who you ask.

components:
  - Board of twelve squares arranged in a circle.
  - Three “crows” per player (bone or shell tokens).
  - Two six-sided dice.

rules_casual: |
  1. Each player places their three crows at start.
  2. Roll 2d6 to move one crow clockwise.
  3. Landing on an occupied space lets you “snatch” the other crow (remove it).
  4. Goal: control the final square, known as “the roost.”

variants_high_stakes:
  - Buy-ins: each crow has a wagered coin value.
  - Bluff tokens: fake crows can be introduced to confuse opponents.
  - Sudden forfeits: players may risk all crows on a single gamble roll.

digital_potential: |
  The rules are simple, quick, and tense—ideal for adaptation as a browser mini-game.
  Both casual play and gambling modes could be implemented, maintaining the same sense
  of risk and reward that defines the tabletop version.
