# Overview
A popular street and tavern game, exclusive to Black Brine. Played casually with chalk circles and seashells, or with carved bone tokens and fine boards in gambling dens.

# Components
- Board of 12 squares in a circle.
- Each player has 3 “crows” (bone or shell tokens).
- 2 six-sided dice.

# Rules (Casual Version)
1. Each player places their 3 crows at start.
2. Roll 2d6 to move one crow clockwise.
3. Landing on an occupied space lets you “snatch” the other crow (remove it).
4. Goal: control the final square (“the roost”).

# Variants (High Stakes)
- Buy-ins: each crow has a wagered value.
- Bluff tokens: fake crows can be introduced.
- Sudden forfeits: players risk all crows on a gamble roll.

# Digital Potential
The rules are simple, fast, and favor tension — perfect for a browser mini-game with both casual play and gambling modes.
