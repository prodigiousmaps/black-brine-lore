# Black Brine — Resin Harvest & Refinement

## Overview
Resin is a core strategic resource in Black Brine. Harvested from brine-adapted and mist-touched growths, it is refined into fuels, sealants, and alchemical compounds critical to the city’s economy.

## Appearance
Harvest zones are dense, twisted groves of mangroves and brine pines, often shrouded in mist. Refinement yards feature vats, stills, and hardened channels stained with dark amber residue.

## Role in Society
Resin underpins maritime survival—sealing hulls, preserving materials, and fueling industry. Its production is tightly licensed, and control of resin flow equates to economic leverage.

## Features
- **Resin Harvesting:** Extracted from mangroves, brine pines, and mist-altered flora in controlled zones.
- **Hazard Conditions:** Toxic fumes, unstable growths, and environmental anomalies are common risks.
- **Refinement Yards:** Distillation, clarification, and grading into industrial and alchemical forms.
- **Product Grades:** Fuels, waterproofing agents, binding compounds, incense, and reagents.
- **Trade Control:** All refined resin is inspected, stamped, and classified before export.

## Notable Figures
- **Inspector of Resins (Trade Authority):** Certifies grades and enforces handling and export regulations.

## Rumors
- Certain resin batches exhibit unpredictable or arcane side effects.
- Illegal refineries operate outside sanctioned zones, producing unstable compounds.
- Some harvest crews report the trees “bleeding” before they are cut.
