# Black Brine — Manufacturing & Industry

## Overview
Black Brine’s industry prioritizes refinement over production. Workshops and yards transform unstable, rare, or salvaged materials into reliable, export-ready goods.

## Appearance
Industrial zones are dense and functional—resin vats, curing racks, ropewalks, and low, reinforced workshops. Surfaces are stained with salt, pitch, and chemical residue.

## Role in Society
Manufacturing converts the island’s hazardous resources into controlled value. Licensing determines who can operate, and industrial output is closely tied to Trade Authority quotas.

## Features
- **Resin Refineries:** Distillation and grading of raw resin into fuels, sealants, and reagents.
- **Mist Glass Workshops:** Small, controlled facilities producing lenses, panes, and arcane implements.
- **Preservation Houses:** Large-scale salting, curing, pressing, and distillation operations.
- **Shipyards & Repair Docks:** Hull patching, resin sealing, reinforcement, and refits.
- **Cooperage & Ropewalks:** High-output production of barrels, rigging, and maritime supplies.
- **Salvage Refit Yards:** Reworking wrecked materials into usable tools and components.

## Notable Figures
- **Master of Licenses (Trade Authority):** Grants and revokes industrial permits, effectively controlling who is allowed to produce.

## Rumors
- Some refineries operate illegal night shifts to process unstable resin grades.
- Mist glass artisans guard techniques that can alter perception or memory.
- Salvage yards occasionally recover items that are quietly removed by Authority agents.
