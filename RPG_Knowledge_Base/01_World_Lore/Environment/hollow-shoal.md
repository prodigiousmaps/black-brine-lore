# Hollow Shoal Island

## Overview
Hollow Shoal Island lies west of Sedna Island within the Twelvefold Veil Archipelago. It is less an island in the conventional sense and more a sprawling network of reefs, sandbars, and half-submerged rock formations that barely rise above the sea.

The island is avoided by most navigators, as charts are unreliable and tides behave unpredictably. Those who linger report strange sightings along the shoals—shapes in the fog, pale eyes above the waterline, and the feeling of being silently observed.

## Appearance
At low tide, Hollow Shoal reveals jagged coral ridges, broken hulls, and ancient stone fragments protruding from the surf. At high tide, much of the island vanishes entirely, leaving only rippling water, foam, and mist.

The sea around the shoal is unnaturally quiet. Waves break without rhythm, and sound seems to travel poorly across the water.

## Role in the Region
Hollow Shoal serves as a natural graveyard for ships entering the Veil from the west. Salvagers from Sedna occasionally risk expeditions here, but few stay long.

The island has no permanent settlements, though temporary camps, signal fires, and wreck-shelters are sometimes found—often abandoned in haste.

## Features
- Extensive reef systems capable of shredding hulls without warning
- Numerous shipwrecks from multiple eras, many unclaimed
- Shallow waters that distort depth perception and navigation magic
- Frequent sightings of mist-formed creatures watching from rocks or rigging

## Notable Figures
- None confirmed; rumors persist of lighthouse keepers or reef-dwellers, but no accounts agree

## Rumors
- The reefs conceal hollow chambers beneath the stone, flooded but navigable at certain tides
- The “watchers” seen in the mist are not native creatures, but reflections from elsewhere
- Ships wrecked here are sometimes found fully intact days later, crew missing, cargo untouched
