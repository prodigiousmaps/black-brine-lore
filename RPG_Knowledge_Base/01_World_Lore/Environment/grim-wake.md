# Grim Wake Island

## Overview
Grim Wake Island rises sharply from the Trackless Sea as a spine of black stone and broken cliffs. Unlike Hollow Shoal, which hides beneath the waves, Grim Wake announces itself violently—through crashing surf, sudden squalls, and treacherous winds that seem to pull ships toward it.

Sailors believe that merely passing within sight of Grim Wake marks a vessel with bad luck. Storms follow in its wake, compasses drift, and tempers fray among the crew.

## Appearance
The island is dominated by steep cliffs, narrow coves, and towering rock formations resembling splintered masts and grasping hands. Waves break constantly against the stone, sending salt spray high into the air.

Little vegetation grows here. What plant life exists is twisted and wind-scoured, clinging to cracks in the rock.

## Role in the Region
Grim Wake acts as both a warning and a boundary within the Twelvefold Veil Archipelago. Captains use it as a grim landmark—proof that they have sailed too far west or strayed from safer routes.

Wrecks accumulate in the coves and at the base of the cliffs, forming a partial ring of broken hulls visible at low tide. Some are ancient, others recent.

## Features
- Constantly rough seas and unpredictable winds
- Jagged cliffs that prevent easy landing
- Numerous shipwrecks, some lashed together by storms
- Strange echoes and horn-like sounds carried by the wind

## Notable Figures
- None confirmed; stories speak of a “Wake Caller,” but descriptions vary wildly

## Rumors
- The island amplifies grief, anger, and fear among those nearby
- Bells sometimes toll from the cliffs during storms, though no structures are visible
- Ships that salvage from Grim Wake often suffer disasters weeks later, far from the island
