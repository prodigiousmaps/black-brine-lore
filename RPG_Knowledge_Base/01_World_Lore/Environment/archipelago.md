# Twelvefold Veil Archipelago

## Overview
The Twelvefold Veil Archipelago is a chain of twelve mapped islands and numerous smaller, unmapped islets scattered across a fog-heavy region of the Trackless Sea. Persistent mist, shifting currents, and inconsistent navigation define the region, giving rise to the name “the Veil.”

The archipelago is widely believed to be shaped not only by weather, but by lingering effects of an ancient divine conflict tied to sea powers now lost or obscured.

## Appearance
From a distance, the islands appear intermittently through fog banks, often seeming closer or farther than they truly are. Coastlines are irregular and difficult to chart, with reefs and shoals that shift subtly over time. Even clear days carry an oppressive haze.

Lantern lights, wreckage, and false horizons are common sights reported by sailors approaching the Veil.

## Role in Society
To outsiders, the Twelvefold Veil is a dangerous and unnecessary risk. To those who dwell within it—particularly on Sedna Island—it is a bounded world, shaped by tradition, superstition, and necessity.

The archipelago exists outside formal political control, though Sedna Island functions as a de facto center of trade, religion, and governance for the region.

## Features
- Twelve officially mapped islands, with additional unmapped islets and reefs
- Persistent fog and weather patterns that resist mundane and magical clearing
- Unreliable navigation, including shifting bearings and disputed charts
- Near-perfect east–west alignment between Sedna Island and the Moonshae island of Flamsterd

## Notable Figures
- None formally recognized at the archipelago level; authority is localized to individual islands

## Rumors
- The Veil is not weather, but a boundary meant to contain something beneath the sea
- Maps of the archipelago subtly change when copied or redrawn
- Ships lost in the Veil sometimes reappear years later, crewed by no one
